## Full description
Most maps tell you the fastest road. Gemini Maps tells you the best road today.

Drivers report potholes, waterlogging, accidents and debris with a photo or a voice command; AI classifies the hazard and every route is ranked against it.

• Say "Hey Gemini" — the whole app runs by voice: navigate, start trip, how far, speed limit, any hazards ahead, report pothole, zoom, switch vehicle.
• Hazard-aware routes: time, distance, fuel cost and a road-safety score for every alternative.
• Vehicle-aware: a pothole matters more to a bike or sedan than an SUV.
• Guardian agent: if a new critical hazard appears ahead mid-drive, it checks alternatives and reroutes with safety checks (no U-turns, no zig-zagging, no big detours).
• Live trip dashboard: km left, time left, arrival time, fuel used, speed and the road's speed limit.
• Fuel cost by Indian state, for petrol, diesel, bike and EV.
• 3D map with clustered hazard pins that stay locked to the road.

Notes: speed limits are typical limits from map data and may differ from signs — always obey posted signs. Fuel prices are estimates. Hazard reports are crowdsourced.
