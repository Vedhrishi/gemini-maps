Gemini Maps — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Gemini Maps is a hazard-aware navigation app built for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon. This policy explains what it does with your data.

Location

If you allow location access, your position is used on your device to show you on the map, to follow a drive, and to match your speed to the road's limit. It is not sent to our servers and not stored. To plan a route, the start and end coordinates are sent to the public OSRM routing service; place searches are sent to the Photon geocoder (Komoot). Those services receive only the coordinates or search text needed for the request.

Microphone and voice

Voice control uses your browser's or device's speech recognition. Audio is processed by that speech service (for example Google or Apple); Gemini Maps does not record or keep audio. You can switch voice off at any time.

Camera and hazard reports

When you report a hazard, the photo you take and its location are sent to the Gemini Maps backend for AI analysis and shown to other drivers as a hazard report. Do not photograph people or number plates. Reports can be removed on request.

Data kept on your device

The app stores small preferences (chosen vehicle, a cache of state names for fuel prices, and map tiles) in your device's local storage. You can clear them by clearing site data.

What we do not do

We do not sell data, show ads, or build advertising profiles. We do not require an account.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
